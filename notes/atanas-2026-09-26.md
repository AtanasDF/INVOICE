# What Atanas raised — 2026-09-26

His words, and what each one means for the app. Nothing here is started unless
it says so.

## 1. The file library on the dashboard — HIS DESIGN, WAIT

*"maybe do not hide it but we need to make the dates a bit smaller and rearrange
it a bit... I'll create the way I want it to look with the help of another chat
and then I'll show you pictures so you can do it the way I like it."*

**Not to be touched until he sends pictures.** I had suggested hiding it on a
brand new account, where it fills a screen with "0 of 0" and "No pictures that
year"; he does not want it hidden, he wants it reworked, and he is designing it
himself. The measurement that started it: a new account's dashboard is 4.1
screens tall and the library sits second.

## 2. Dark mode walkthrough frames — APPROVED, DOING

*"if you think is good let's do it."*

Today the frames are always recorded light, so somebody on a dark phone gets six
bright white rectangles. The reasoning written down for that ("a dark frame shown
to somebody whose phone is light is a picture of a different app") cuts both ways.

The cost is smaller than it first looks: frames are loaded per journey, and a
viewer only ever loads the set matching their own theme, so **nobody downloads
more than they do now** — only the repo and the deploy carry both sets.

## 3. Signing in does not use Touch ID — TO LOOK AT

*"When log in it doesn't directly ask you to prove it's you via finger print via
the mac and sends you to a different page to prove it's you."*

Two things tangled together, and worth separating before building anything:
- **No passkey / Touch ID.** Sign-in is email and password. A passkey (WebAuthn)
  is what makes the Mac ask for a fingerprint, and Supabase Auth does not do it
  natively — it needs building against a credential store.
- **"sends you to a different page"** — the six-digit code screen. Needs watching
  live to see which step he means and whether it fires when it should not.

## 4. Payments, and accounts talking to each other — MY ANSWER: LATER

*"Communication between profiles for exchange information between accounts
automatically and requesting, receiving and sending payments... is it a good idea
for now or should we leave it for later?"*

**Later, and the two halves are not the same size.**

- **Moving money between people is a regulated activity.** Holding it, even
  briefly, is the part that needs permission, and building it means Stripe or
  similar doing the holding and the app never touching it. That is weeks, and it
  is the piece that can go legally wrong rather than just technically wrong.
- **Accounts talking to each other is a bigger change than it sounds**: today
  every row is scoped to one account, and RLS is one rule — `auth.uid() = user_id`.
  The moment an invoice can be read by the person it was sent to, that rule has to
  become "mine, or shared with me", and every table, policy and query is in scope.
  That is the sort of change that leaks other people's data when it is rushed.
- **What already does most of it**: a customer gets a private `/i/` link and can
  pay from the details on the invoice; a supplier answers a quote request at `/r/`
  without an account at all. Neither needs profiles to talk.

**Recommendation: ship without it.** Revisit when there are enough accounts that
two of them actually know each other — and do the money half through Stripe, which
is already on the launch plan for week 2.

## 5. Security once people keep confidential company information — NOTED, FUTURE

His point, and it is right. What exists today: RLS on every table, private storage
with signed URLs, no service key in the browser, Turnstile on the front door.
What a serious pass would add: a look at what an account can reach that is not
theirs, and at what is in the logs.

## 6. Scanner is slow — INVESTIGATING FIRST

*"Scanner is replying very slow now. Takes ages to start actually scanning."*

The most concrete thing on the list and the feature he uses most, so it goes
first. Two separate stopwatches: how long from tapping Scan to the camera being
live, and how long from the shutter to the reading coming back.

## 7. Scan a document that is not from one of his suppliers — TO BUILD

*"The system should allow you to scan documents from company that isn't theirs
and the system should be also able to process (send or download the file) without
saving a new company to the account."*

Today `/copy` photographs any paper and makes a PDF to save, share or email,
storing nothing — which is most of this. What he is describing is the same thing
reachable from the scanner, so a document that is nobody's supplier can be dealt
with and sent on without a contact being created. `receipts.details.noSupplier`
already marks "No supplier" chosen on purpose.
